# Legal Education Without Borders
### An initiative by Cambridge Law Academy

**Language should never be a barrier to legal knowledge, professional opportunity or access to justice.**

We are developing AI-powered multilingual educational tools to make the law of England and Wales accessible to internationally trained legal professionals and aspiring lawyers.

Our mission is to:

- Overcome linguistic and financial barriers to legal education.
- Contribute to better access to justice and more affordable legal services.
- Promote understanding of fundamental rights, judicial independence and the rule of law.
- Build educational and professional bridges between nations and cultures.
- Make specialist legal knowledge more accessible through responsible AI innovation.

Our initial focus is Russian-speaking communities, with plans to expand into other languages.

**Project status:** Active development. This newly established repository is a public introduction to the initiative. No open-source software has been released here yet. We are exploring the future publication of selected reusable educational tools and methodologies.
